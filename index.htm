Privacy and safety

The checkers run in your browser. They do not send submitted text or links to a Guardian server, and they do not follow the links you enter.

Guardian uses no accounts or advertising trackers in this prototype. Do not enter sensitive information.

If you are worried about a bank, account or payment, contact the organisation independently through a trusted app or telephone number.

Important: A link or message with no detected warning signs may still be malicious.
